Port Brief Issue 1, Monday 12 October 2026
 

Gales likely to stop cranes at Liverpool on Tuesday as Atlantic storms build

Liverpool faces its worst day on Tuesday, with gusts to 22 m/s from 10:00 to 22:00. Severe to extreme seas across the North Atlantic will hold up ships from North America by 2 days or more, and the Western Approaches turn severe on Wednesday.

The ports this week
United Kingdom
Felixstowe    Watch
A short gusty spell on Monday evening from 20:00 to 21:00, then settled. Allow 12–24 hours on anything due off the quay tonight and push releases through for the rest of the week.
Southampton    Watch
Brief gusts near crane limits on Monday and Tuesday mornings. Avoid tight cut-offs on those mornings and allow 12–24 hours.
London Gateway    Running normally
No crane or pilotage limits forecast all week. A good week to clear releases: speak to your carrier about release and delivery now.
Liverpool    Disruption likely
Cranes are likely to stop on Tuesday from 10:00 to 22:00, with gusts to 22 m/s. Tell your haulier today, move Tuesday collections to Wednesday and allow 24–48 hours.
North Europe
Rotterdam    Watch
Short spells near crane limits on Tuesday afternoon and again on Saturday. If you tranship here, allow 12–24 hours on weekend feeder connections.
Antwerp    Watch
Gusts near crane limits on Tuesday afternoon, then from Friday evening through Saturday. Allow 12–24 hours on Friday and weekend moves.
Bremerhaven    Watch
Windy from Tuesday night into Wednesday morning, and again on Saturday. Allow 12–24 hours on Wednesday vessel calls and avoid tight cut-offs.
Hamburg    Watch
Gusty on Tuesday and from Tuesday evening to Wednesday 10:00, then again on Saturday. Allow 12–24 hours and keep Wednesday morning plans flexible.
