Port Brief Issue 12, Sunday 11 October 2026
 

Felixstowe congested as wind threatens London Gateway on Wednesday

Disruption likely: London Gateway and Hamburg. Congested: Felixstowe and Bremerhaven. Watch: Liverpool and Rotterdam. Rough seas in the Bay of Biscay may delay Asia, Mediterranean and West Africa services into the UK by 1–2 days. Reliability: Sea-Intelligence 49.9%, Xeneta 29%.

On the way in
Two storms matter for European arrivals. Biscay turns severe on Wednesday and Thursday, so services from Asia, the Mediterranean and West Africa into the UK are likely to run 1–2 days late. Ships rounding the Cape on Friday may lose up to 1 day. Don't book haulage against the published ETA.
