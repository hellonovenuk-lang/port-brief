Hamburg and Shanghai gusts build into Monday as reliability slides below 50%

UK gateways look workable through the weekend, with Liverpool's gusts easing from Thursday. The weather risk shifts to Hamburg and Shanghai on Monday 5 October. August reliability fell again: Sea-Intelligence 49.9% (down 5.9 pp), Xeneta 29%.
