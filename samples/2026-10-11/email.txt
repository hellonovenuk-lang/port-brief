PORT BRIEF
Issue 1, Sunday 11 October 2026

Two windy spells to plan around at UK and North Sea ports this week

Gusts near crane limits hit Felixstowe and Southampton on Monday and return across the Channel and North Sea from Friday. A deep Atlantic low will hold up ships from North America by 1–2 days. Maersk lifts its UK fuel surcharge to 20%.

THE PORTS THIS WEEK

Felixstowe: Watch
Gusts to 16 m/s from 12:00 to 21:00 on Monday may pause cranes. Move Monday afternoon collections to Tuesday morning if you can, and allow 12–24 hours on anything booked tight.

Southampton: Watch
Two short gusty spells on Monday and Tuesday, then a longer one on Friday from 10:00 to 22:00. Keep Friday bookings flexible and allow 12–24 hours.

London Gateway: Watch
The worst of the week here: gusts to 19 m/s from Friday 16:00 to Saturday 14:00. Tell your haulier now and move Friday evening and Saturday collections to early Friday or Monday.

Liverpool: Running normally
No crane or pilotage limits forecast all week. A good week to clear releases: speak to your carrier about release and delivery now.

Rotterdam: Watch
An hour near crane limits on Tuesday evening, then a gustier spell from Saturday 14:00. If you tranship here, confirm weekend feeder connections and allow 12–24 hours.

Antwerp: Watch
Brief gusts on Tuesday afternoon, then Friday 13:00 to 20:00 and again from Saturday afternoon. Allow 12–24 hours on Friday and weekend moves.

Bremerhaven: Watch
Gusty again on Tuesday from 09:00 to 13:00 and late on Saturday. Allow 12–24 hours on Tuesday vessel calls and avoid tight cut-offs.

Hamburg: Watch
Gusts near crane limits on Tuesday from 08:00 to 15:00 and late on Saturday. Allow 12–24 hours on Tuesday and keep weekend plans flexible.

Congestion figures were not available for this issue, so statuses reflect weather forecasts and port news only.

ON THE WAY IN
A deep low in the mid-North Atlantic brings seas of 6 m and gusts near 30 m/s on Tuesday and Wednesday. Ships from North America crossing in that window are likely to arrive 1–2 days late. Don't book haulage against the published ETA; confirm the revised ETA with your carrier before you commit.
